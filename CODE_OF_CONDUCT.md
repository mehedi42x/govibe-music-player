# Code of Conduct

GoVibe welcomes participation from people with varied backgrounds and experience. We expect everyone to make the project a respectful, constructive space.

Examples of expected behavior include communicating with empathy, giving actionable feedback, respecting differing viewpoints, and focusing criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Apply moderation consistently and proportionately. For a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers through a private route configured in the repository; do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

This policy applies to project spaces, pull requests, issues, and project-related community interactions. Maintainers should publish a safe private reporting route before actively soliciting community participation.
